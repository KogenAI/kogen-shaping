# Stop reading the removed opt-ins in shaping

Slug: k-shape-drop-optins
Model: not applicable — each removed setting becomes its fixed default; no state, retry or interacting rule is added
Acceptance:
- .kogen/intents/k-shape-drop-optins/acceptance.feature

- features/steps/k_shape_drop_optins_test.go.txt

K-SHAPE-DROP-OPTINS. Under the remove-now ruling (no options or knobs, and findings are hard-coded), the coordinator is removing its opt-in settings: `shape_workers`, `shape_audit`, `shape_decision_check` and `review_material`, plus `max_rounds` on every role. Its candidates k-drop-optins and k-coord-reject-max-rounds then refuse a `.kogen/project.yaml` that sets any of them (`environment/project_config_invalid`). For now they keep the exported fields at fixed values: ShapeWorkers, ShapeAudit and ShapeDecisionCheck always off, and review material always "parts". kogen-shaping still reads these settings, and its tests write the keys, so against either candidate the suite fails with `project_config_invalid`. That blocks both coordinator landings (consumer check, 12:52Z and 13:21Z).

After this change, kogen-shaping (packages `shape` and `cmd/kogen-shape`) reads none of these settings and always behaves as with today's defaults:
- no shape workers and no `shape_batch` tool;
- no request audit;
- no decision check;
- for a claude-code shaper, the fixed external round cap.

That behaviour is hard-coded. No option, flag, setting or environment variable is added. Code that only these opt-ins could reach is deleted, along with its tests. Every remaining test asserts what it asserts now. No file under `shape/` or `cmd/` writes or names a removed key, comments included. The module builds, vets and passes `go test ./...` both against the coordinator it pins today (v0.0.0-20261102193107-c4a49a4fe84f, which still parses the keys) and against a coordinator that rejects the keys and drops the fields.

Kogen-shaping references none of these coordinator identifiers: `project.Role.MaxRounds`, `project.DefaultExternalRounds`, `project.Config.ShapeWorkers`, `project.Workers`, `project.Config.ShapeAudit`, `project.Audit`, `project.Config.ShapeDecisionCheck`, `project.DecisionCheck`, `project.Config.ReviewMaterial` and `project.ReviewMaterial`. Shaping never read `review_material`. For it, the change is only that no test or fixture writes it.

What changes for a project that still sets an option on today's coordinator: shaping ignores the value.
- A `models.shape.max_rounds` no longer caps a claude-code shaper.
- `shape_workers` enabled no longer offers `shape_batch` or adds worker roles. With a claude-code shaper, it is no longer refused with "shape workers are not supported…".
- `shape_audit` and `shape_decision_check` enabled no longer run an audit or a decision check.

Out of scope: go.mod and go.sum keep their pins. Also unchanged are kogen-coordinator, this repository's `.kogen/project.yaml`, the `features/steps` files of earlier Intents, the CLI and its flags, and every timeout.

## Decisions
- Claude-code round cap: a claude-code Shape or Refine session's round cap is the executor's own shape-role `MaxRounds` (`Descriptor.For(executor.RoleShape)`), or 6 when that is 0. This is exactly today's result for a role that sets no `max_rounds`. The 6 becomes a kogen-shaping constant, because `project.DefaultExternalRounds` is one of the removed identifiers. `executor.Task.MaxRounds` carries the cap, and Kogen's own round limit (`candidate/executor_round_limit`) applies it. (source: request)
- No shape workers or batch tool: the native shaper's tools are always today's default list, in order: `read_file, view_image, list_files, search_text, probe, documentation, propose_files, submit`. A `shape_batch` call is answered `error: unknown tool shape_batch`, as today without workers. Delete the batch and worker machinery: shape/batch.go's jobs, dispatch, workers and their bounds; the `ShapeBatchTool` and `WorkerResultTool` names and definitions; the worker tool lists; the `batchUsed` state; and the `shape_batch` branch of the tool loop. (source: request)
- `ShapeRoles(cfg *project.Config) []provider.Role` keeps its name and signature. It returns only `models.shape` for a native shaper and nothing for an external one. Delete `requireShapeRoute` and its refusal ("shape workers are not supported with executor … disable shape_workers or use the native shaper"): with no workers, nothing is left to refuse. (source: request)
- No request audit: delete the audit call and its session (native and external), the audit block in the context, the audit coverage gate (the `submission_refused` gate `audit`), and the `request_audit` events. Also delete the audit tool, schema, instructions and parser, `defectTemplates` and `uncoveredDefects`, and everything else only they use. Keep the per-run `unresolved.json` record: each ready Shape run still writes `.kogen/local/shaping/<slug>/unresolved.json` with exactly `[]`. Keep the approval card's "Unresolved request conflicts" lines (`unresolvedLines`), which read that record, because records of earlier runs can remain in a checkout and each ready run replaces them. (source: request)
- No decision check: delete the decision check call and its sessions (native and external), its gate (the `submission_refused` gate `decision_check`), the `decision_check` and `decision_assumptions_marked` events, and the automatic assumption marking (`markAssumptions`). Also delete the decision check tool, schema, instructions, parser and outcome rules, and everything else only they use. The approval card's "Assumptions to confirm" lines (`assumptionLines`) stay, because they read the Intent text, which can carry marks from earlier runs. Keep the helpers that other code uses (`decisionLines`, `sourceTagRe`, `normalizeWhitespace`, `containsNormalized`, `mustJSON`, `unresolvedFile`, `unresolvedLines`, `assumptionLines`); they may move to another file. (source: request)
- The usage lines are unchanged: today's default audit and decision check terms are zero. A claude-code line counts the rounds of the main, contract and question sessions; a native line sums the same usages. (source: request)
- Model-facing bytes stay byte-identical: every prompt under shape/prompts, `ShaperInstructions`, the Refine prompt, the default and Refine tool definitions, and the external session note (`externalNote`). The note's list of native tools a claude-code session lacks still names `shape_batch`. (source: request)
- Goldens: shape/testdata/defs_golden.json drops exactly the `shape_batch` and `worker_result` entries. shape/testdata/shaper_policy_golden.json drops exactly the `workers-enabled`, `read`, `probe` and `draft` tool lists. Every other entry stays semantically unchanged, and prompts_golden.json and both force goldens stay byte-identical. Regenerate with `go test ./... -update`, then confirm the diff is only those removals. (source: request)
- Tests stop writing and naming the removed keys, comments included. Tests that set a round cap use the fake executor's shape-role capability. Delete the tests of deleted behaviour; every other test keeps its assertions. (source: request)
- The acceptance overlay relies on these existing test helpers keeping their names and signatures. Fixture: `newShpFx`; the `shpFx` fields `Root`, `S`, `Stdout`, `Stderr`, `Client` and `Prov`, and its methods `write`, `read`, `exists`, `git`, `writeLineup`, `setReport`, `shape`, `newSession` and `events`. Fake provider: the `shpProvider` field `route` and method `requests`. Scripted steps and expectations: `shpStep`, `shpAnswer`, `shpCall`, `shpSubmit`, `shpFiles`, `shpToolNames`, `shpOutputs`, `shpNamed`, `shpScopedIntentMD`, `shpFeature`, `shpSteps` and `shpSlug`. Fake executor: the `shapeExec` fields `t`, `rounds`, `starts` and `inputs`, its `Describe` method (a descriptor with an `executor.RoleShape` entry), and `result`. Entry points: `Shape`, the `ShapeInput` fields `Project`, `Slug`, `Request`, `Client` and `External`, and `ShapeRoles`. (source: request)
- Only owned child processes and temporary folders are used. Tests are synthetic only, with no live provider. (source: request)

## Feasibility
- The synthetic coordinator is the pinned module copied to a temporary folder. In its non-test Go files, the nine removed identifiers become unexported and the five removed keys' `case` labels become unknown. String literals such as the config digest's "shape_workers" atom are unchanged, so provenance bytes stay the same. This qualifies the deeper assertions that the external red masks. The same variant with a wrong expected cap fails at the round-cap assertion.
